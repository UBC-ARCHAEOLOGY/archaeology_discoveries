Caption: Fig. 3. Familial pedigree groups identified from ancient genomes and the site plan for the Ust’-Ida I cemetery. Open in a new tab Individuals detected for plague are marked with bacilli silhouettes. Pedigrees are drawn from 30 sampled individuals at Ust’-Ida I and 8 from Bratskii Kamen; only close familial relationships are shown; although many other third- or fourth-degree relationships are detected at Ust’-Ida I (see Supplementary Note 2 ). Pedigrees are reconstructed based on deaths occurring contemporaneously. The two samples from Serovo (not shown) were found to be fourth-degree relatives. y.o., years old.
