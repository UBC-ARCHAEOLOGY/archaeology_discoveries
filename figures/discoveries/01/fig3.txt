Caption: Download: PNG larger image TIFF original image Fig 3. (a) The upper picture shows black and white luminescence images of samples illuminated with a blue light lamp (455 nm) filtered with a long-wave pass filter (530 nm): (1) Unburnt fossil bone (2) burnt fossil bone and (3) fluoridated bone (diagenetic effects during fossilisation) all of them from Wonderwerk Cave site (see Methods section). (b) Graph of spectral emission of the same samples: Their spectral content is highly different, especially prominent for the burnt fossil bone (2), lower in the fluoridated fossil bone (3) and the lowest luminescence is provided by the unburnt fossil bone (1). (c) FTIR spectra of fluoridated vs non-fluoridated bones. Bone is fluoridated by the relative height ratios between the peak at ~565 cm-1 and 605 cm-1. If the 565 peak is taller than the 605 peak, the bone is not fluoridated (red spectrum R26 10a 5–10 White Bone 2). If the 605 peak is taller than the 565 peak, the bone is fluoridated (Blue spectrum R26 10a 5–10 White Bone 3). https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0347480.g003
